This project is built with "Student" as main target user.


Students often have a lot on their plate to learn and upgrade themselves.
They often have large goals in their brain.
Our goal is to decompose that big goals into small achievable milestones.


For instance,

~ A student's goal is to get an internship.
~ But don't know:
    -where to start
    -what milestones are required
    -which tasks depend on others
    -how much time each task requires
    -what to do each day
    -how to recover when they fall behind
~ Goal decomposer should: A goal-decomposition agent that converts a high-level goal into structured 
                                    -milestones, 
                                    -tasks, 
                                    -dependencies and 
                                    -an achievable schedule, 
                          then continuously adjusts the plan based on user progress.
